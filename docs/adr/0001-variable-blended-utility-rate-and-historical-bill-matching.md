# Variable Blended Utility Rate and Historical Bill Matching

We calculate Home Charging electricity cost using a **Variable Blended Rate** (`(Total Bill Amount - Fixed Monthly Charges) / Total Billed kWh`) extracted from uploaded **Utility Bills** and matched chronologically against each **Billing Period**, falling back to the most recent bill's **Effective Rate** (or a configurable `$0.16/kWh` default) for dates without a covering bill. Fixed customer/connection fees are excluded because the household incurs them regardless of EV ownership, while full Time-of-Use (TOU) tariff schedule reconstruction was rejected because residential charger exports and utility bills rarely share uniform TOU tier timestamps (users on TOU plans can instead override the derived rate in the bill confirmation sheet).
